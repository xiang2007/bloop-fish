Fisheye: Product Requirements

A phone-first pond logbook for workers and a decision dashboard for owners, replacing the farm's monthly feed and mortality spreadsheets.

Date: 2026-10-05 · Type: retroactive PRD, written from the codebase at 8fc03be · Format: HTML (embeds diagrams) · Supersedes: none · Detail lives in ARCHITECTURE.md and database.md. Items tagged [ASSUMPTION] are not confirmed by the repo or the README.

1. Problem statement

The farm tracks each pond in a spreadsheet: daily feed type and bags, dead fish, monthly cost tables and an average-weight summary (README, "Overview"). The sheet is hand-totalled, so it carries errors the app has already met in the sample data: mislabelled month headings, a cost table copied from the previous month, and bags costed at the wrong pack size (README, "Spreadsheet import"). Owners cannot see across ponds, cannot tell when a pond is ready to harvest, and cannot tell whether feed will run out. Workers do the data entry on Android phones, where a spreadsheet is a poor fit.

Initial scope is 7 ponds and about 10,000 kg of fish. The long-term goal is AI-assisted recommendations, starting with feed. That depends on clean, structured history first.

2. Goals and non-goals

Goals

  • G1. A worker records one pond's day (feed, bags, dead fish) on a phone in a single screen.
  • G2. The owner sees stock, feed cost, growth, FCR, survival and harvest readiness for every pond without opening a spreadsheet.
  • G3. The owner is warned about problems (logging gaps, mortality spikes, sampling due, low feed, poor water) by explicit rules.
  • G4. Existing spreadsheet history is imported and reconciled, not retyped.
  • G5. Cost history stays true: later price changes never rewrite past records.

Non-goals (v1)

  • Predictive or AI feed recommendations (the README keeps this as a later phase; today's insights are plain rules).
  • Real authentication (see risk R1).
  • A native mobile app, offline mode, water-quality sensor integration, payroll or sales and revenue tracking.

3. Users and stories

WhoStory
WorkerAs a worker on an Android phone, I pick a pond from today's checklist, enter feed type, bags and dead fish, and save, so the owner has today's numbers without me touching a spreadsheet.
WorkerAs a worker, I weigh a sample of fish and enter fish count and total weight, so the pond's average weight stays current.
WorkerAs a worker, I correct a mistake I made earlier this week, but cannot rewrite old history.
OwnerAs the owner, I open the overview and see which ponds are ready to harvest, which need attention, and this month's feed cost against last month's.
OwnerAs the owner, I record a partial or final harvest by size grade, so cycle results and harvest totals are real figures.
OwnerAs the owner, I record a stocktake or a delivery and see days of feed cover per feed type.
OwnerAs the owner, I upload the old Pond report CSV and see what did not add up before I trust it, and I can roll the import back.

4. Requirements

Each requirement names the current behaviour so it can be tested. IDs are referenced in the figures below.

Must-have (v1: built and in the product)

IDRequirement and acceptance test
M1Role split. A visitor chooses Farm owner or Farm worker. A worker is redirected to /log from every other path. Every owner-only action rejects a worker with "Only farm owners can do this", even when posted directly.
M2Daily log. One record per pond per day (feed type, bags, dead fish, note). Saving the same pond and day again updates the record. Bags 0 to 200; dead fish a whole number 0 to 100,000; bags need a feed type; date not in the future.
M3Worker edit window. A worker can save or delete only records within the last 7 days, and delete only their own app entries. The owner can edit any date.
M4Cost snapshot. On save, feedKg = bags × pack size and feedCostRm = feedKg × price at that moment. Changing a feed price afterwards leaves existing records unchanged.
M5Sampling. Enter 1 to 10,000 fish and 0 to 10,000 kg; average weight is stored as total ÷ count.
M6Pond metrics, per running cycle only. Estimated biomass (feed ÷ assumed FCR), survival, realized FCR, daily gain, feed cost per kg, 7-day feed rate and monthly rows. Records from earlier cycles never enter the running totals.
M7Cycle lifecycle. States unset, growing, ready, empty. Stage by quarter of the planned cycle (default 8 months): nursery, juvenile, growout, finishing. Restock only an empty pond; closing and reopening are transactional.
M8Harvest. Partial harvests with size-grade lines or boxes × box weight (default 500 kg). A harvest flagged final closes the cycle with a totals snapshot. Total loss closes it as "lost" with a required cause. The last closed cycle can be reopened.
M9Health and alerts. Per-pond checks (survival, mortality spike, FCR, growth, logging gap, sampling age, water) and an owner alert list with critical, warning and info levels. Thresholds are those in ARCHITECTURE §9.3 and §9.4.
M10Feed stock. On hand = last stocktake + deliveries − bags logged since. With no stocktake the balance shows as unknown, not zero. Days of cover use the 14-day average. Warning under 7 days, critical under 3.
M11Spreadsheet import. Parse the Pond report CSV (up to 900 KB), reconcile against the sheet's own totals, list findings, skip dates already entered in the app, and allow whole-batch rollback.
M12Owner dashboards. Overview, farm map, pond page (overview, cycle, records, data, settings tabs), harvests, inventory, settings, and a per-pond CSV export.
M13Languages. English, Chinese and Malay for all screens, switchable per browser.
M14Day correctness. Calendar days are keyed in Asia/Kuala_Lumpur so a record never moves to another day.

Should-have (fast follow)

  • S1. Real sign-in with per-person accounts, replacing the role cookie (closes R1).
  • S2. Month report screen: lib/monthReport.ts and ReportPicker exist but are not mounted on any page.
  • S3. Move import_batches.summary/findings from JSON strings to jsonb.
  • S4. Fix .env.example, which still shows a SQLite URL; the app needs PostgreSQL.

Won't-have in this version

  • AI feed recommendations. Rules remain the only decision support until at least one full cycle of clean data exists [ASSUMPTION].
  • Offline entry, push notifications, native app.
  • Multi-tenant (several unrelated farm businesses in one deployment). The 100-pond scenario is a demo of scale for one owner.

7. Open questions and risks

IDItem
R1No real authentication. The role is a cookie chosen on /select-role; anyone who can reach the site can become owner. Acceptable only while the URL is private. Blocks any use with sensitive data. See S1.
R2Estimates depend on assumed FCR. Harvestable kg uses assumedFcr (default 1.35). A wrong value skews biomass and cost per kg until sampling data corrects it. Who owns this value per pond?
R3Survival, sampling biomass and realized FCR need stockedCount. Ponds without it show less, and an alert asks for it.
R4Pages recompute everything per request. Fine at 7 ponds; the 100-pond scenario exists to test this, but no performance target is defined.
R5Open: who is the single owner of thresholds (mortality spike, FCR margin, stock cover days)? They are fixed in code today.
R6Open: what data volume and quality is needed before AI recommendations are credible?
